# XeroWA AI — Master Market Launch Execution Roadmap

**Parent company:** Xero Seven AI
**Primary outcome:** Acquire 10–15 paying Indore SMB clients in the first 30 days after launch.
**Operating principle:** Show, don't tell. Every prospect should be able to message a live XeroWA AI demo, see a useful reply, and book the next step.

This is the execution source of truth for brand, product proof, demand generation, sales, onboarding, payments, and launch operations. Each task has a clear owner, evidence requirement, and launch gate. Do not mark a task complete from intent alone: attach the URL, file, screenshot, test result, customer note, or payment proof that demonstrates completion.

## Operating scoreboard

| Metric | 30-day target | Evidence |
|:--|:--:|:--|
| Paying businesses | 10–15 | Signed order, payment receipt, and activated tenant |
| Qualified demos | 30 | Demo log with source, industry, status, and next action |
| Outreach businesses | 75+ | CRM/worksheet with date, owner, consent/context, and result |
| Demo-to-close rate | 25%+ | Weekly funnel review |
| First-response proof | Under 10 seconds in live demo | Timestamped WhatsApp test |
| Setup-to-live time | Same business day | Onboarding checklist and tenant activation timestamp |
| Client proof assets | 3 usable testimonials/case studies | Approved written or recorded consent |

## Execution rules

- Keep customer-facing branding as XeroWA AI or XeroWA.
- Preserve existing internal database columns, storage keys, environment variables, agent identifiers, and API route URLs during the rebrand.
- Use only approved claims. Label estimates, pilots, and customer-reported outcomes clearly.
- Get opt-in or a defensible business context before sending marketing messages. Honour opt-outs immediately.
- Record the owner, due date, status, evidence link, and next action for every open item.
- Review the scoreboard every evening during the 30-day sprint; make one decision per metric that is below target.
